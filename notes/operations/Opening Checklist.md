# Opening Checklist

The café opens at 7:00 am. The opener arrives at 6:15.

1. Alarm off, lights on, machine on. It needs 25 minutes to heat.
2. Put away the milk delivery. See [[Dairy and Oat Milk Order]].
3. Dial in the espresso: 18 g in, 38 g out, 27 to 30 seconds.
4. Fill the pastry case with the delivery from [[The Daily Loaf Bakery]].
5. Count the float: $200.
6. Write the fridge temperatures in the [[Food Safety Log]].

The opener is on the [[Staff Rota]].
