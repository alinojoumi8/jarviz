# Probat Roaster Maintenance

A 12 kg Probat drum roaster, bought used in 2022.

- Every Tuesday: empty the chaff collector, clean the cooling tray, check the gas pressure.
- Every month: grease the front bearing.
- Every 6 months: the technician cleans the exhaust ducts. Last visit: 9 September. Next: March.
- The drum motor belt was replaced in June for $240.

A blocked exhaust is a fire risk. If the roast time gets longer for no reason, stop and check the ducts. The service cost is in the [[Monthly Budget]].
