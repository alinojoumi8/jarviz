# Closing Checklist

The café closes at 5:00 pm, and at 4:00 pm on Sunday.

1. Backflush the espresso machine with cleaner.
2. Empty and wash the grinder hoppers.
3. Put unsold pastries in the Too Good To Go bags.
4. Cash up and put the takings in the safe.
5. Mop, bins out, alarm on.

Closing takes about 40 minutes with two people. If the till is more than $5 out, write it in the [[Weekly Meeting Notes]].
