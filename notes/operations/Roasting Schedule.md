# Roasting Schedule

Tom roasts three days a week on the Probat.

- Monday: [[House Espresso Blend]], 4 batches of 10 kg.
- Wednesday: House Espresso Blend, 3 batches, plus [[Decaf Colombia]], 1 batch.
- Friday: [[Seasonal Single Origin]], and anything we are short of.
- Wholesale orders close at noon the day before a roast day.

Coffee rests for 5 days before it goes to wholesale customers. Never roast on a Tuesday: that is the day for [[Probat Roaster Maintenance]].
