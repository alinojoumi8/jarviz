# Barista Training

What a new barista learns in the first two weeks.

- Week 1: the till, the [[Opening Checklist]] and the [[Closing Checklist]], milk steaming.
- Week 2: dialling in, the espresso recipe, the drinks menu, tasting.
- Sign-off: 20 flat whites in a row that Maya would serve.
