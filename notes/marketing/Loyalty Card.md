# Loyalty Card

Paper stamp card. Buy nine drinks and the tenth is free.

- About 310 cards are redeemed each month.
- A free drink costs us about $1.30.
- [[Greenline Coworking]] members also get 10% off with their member badge.

Idea: double stamps on weekdays after 2 pm, to fill the slow afternoons. Test it in [[January]] and compare with the [[Q3 Sales Summary]] numbers.
