# House Espresso Blend

Our main blend since 2023. It is in every milk drink we sell, and it is the bag most wholesale customers order.

- Recipe: 60% [[Finca La Esperanza]] washed Colombia, 40% [[Kivu Cooperative]] natural.
- Roast: medium-dark, drop at 218°C, 11 minutes 30 seconds. See the [[Roasting Schedule]].
- Retail price: $18 for 340 g. The wholesale price is on the [[Wholesale Price List]].
- Tasting notes: dark chocolate, toasted hazelnut, a little orange peel.
