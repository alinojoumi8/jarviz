# Decaf Colombia

Sugarcane-process decaf from [[Finca La Esperanza]]. People do not expect decaf to taste this good, so we give out samples at the [[Farmers Market Stall]].

- Roast: medium, drop at 212°C.
- Retail price: $19 for 340 g.
- Sells about 14 kg a month. Slow but steady.
- [[Hotel Marlowe]] takes 4 kg a month for room service.

Reorder when the green stock goes under 20 kg. Lead time is five weeks.
