# Subscription Box

Monthly coffee by post. Started in March 2025.

- 86 active members as of October.
- Price: $32 a month for two 250 g bags, shipping included.
- Ships on the first Tuesday of the month. The boxes come from [[Northside Packaging]].
- Churn is about 5% a month. Most people cancel after month three.
