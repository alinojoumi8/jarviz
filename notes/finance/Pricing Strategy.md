# Pricing Strategy

Why prices go up in January.

- Green coffee costs 11% more than last year. The [[Kivu Cooperative]] lots went up most.
- Wages went up 4% in October.
- We have not raised café prices since March 2024.

The plan:

- Flat white from $4.75 to $5.00. Oat milk stays a free swap.
- Retail bags stay the same price.
- Wholesale goes up about 5%. See the [[Wholesale Price List]].

The risk is [[Hotel Marlowe]]. They are slow to pay and may shop around.
