# Kivu Cooperative

Washing-station co-op on Lake Kivu, Rwanda. We buy through the importer Bridgewater Coffee.

- Natural-process lots. They are 40% of the [[House Espresso Blend]] and the current [[Seasonal Single Origin]].
- The price went up from $9.80 to $11.20 per kg this year.
- About 900 kg a year. The minimum order is one pallet.

The price rise is the reason for the review in the [[Pricing Strategy]] note.
